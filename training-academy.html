Training Academy

A planned multi-level airsoft training program built around safe play, practical teamwork, and organized scenario participation.

Program Overview

Training That Builds on Experience

The Hostile Ground Training Academy is being developed as a progression of training levels for players with different backgrounds and experience. The levels below are a proposed framework, not a finalized course syllabus.

Course topics, requirements, instructors, locations, session lengths, costs (if any), and completion standards will be announced when approved.

Proposed Training Levels

Level I / Foundation

Airsoft Fundamentals

Suggested focus: safe participation, equipment readiness, field communication, and an introduction to organized play.

Course details and dates: to be announced.

Level II / Development

Team Integration

Suggested focus: cooperative play, team coordination, and communication during structured airsoft scenarios.

Course details and dates: to be announced.

Level III / Advanced

Scenario Leadership

Suggested focus: planning, team leadership, responsible decision-making, and after-action learning in simulated game scenarios.

Course details and dates: to be announced.

How Class Enrollment Will Work

  1. Browse the class: Review the approved course description, date, capacity, and any prerequisites once published here.
  2. Open the Discord event: Follow that specific class's event link to view the scheduled session.
  3. Express interest: Use Discord's Interested option to notify organizers and receive event reminders.
  4. Confirm your place: Follow the organizers' instructions for a confirmed seat, check-in, and attendance.

Important: Selecting Interested on a Discord event is not itself a confirmed registration and does not prove attendance. Class capacity, waitlists, and completion records will need organizer confirmation or a dedicated registration system.

Direct links to individual class events will be added after those events are created. No additional website login or collection of participant details is required at this stage.
