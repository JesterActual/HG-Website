Player Intel / Faction Briefing

Factions

LOCKBOX / OPERATIONAL INTELLIGENCE // Three forces converge on a restricted zone. Their orders differ. Their objectives conflict. What each chooses to reveal may matter as much as what each seeks to recover.

ASC / Green

Ares Security Compact

SECURE THE ASSET. CONTROL THE OUTCOME.

Operational Profile

A private security coalition deployed into hostile environments to recover sensitive assets, secure critical components, and control the outcome inside the area of operations. ASC operates with discipline and precision; its personnel are measured by the mission completed, not the ground occupied.

Doctrine // Mission Before Ego

Observe. Orient. Decide. Act. Reconnaissance identifies the opening; direct-action patrols exploit it. Asset recovery secures what matters. Component control denies an opponent the opportunity to use it.

“We work quietly. We strike precisely. We leave no loose ends.”

Primary Operational Focus

  • Recon: Gather intelligence and understand the ground.
  • Direct action: Engage objectives decisively.
  • Asset recovery: Locate, secure, extract and protect sensitive materiel.
  • Component control: Keep critical components away from rival forces.

PCF / Tan

Provisional Control Force

Operational Profile

PCF is the regional military authority asserting control over the operational area. Where rival forces see an opportunity to recover what was lost, PCF sees an unacceptable challenge to its authority.

Doctrine // Hold the Ground

Control the routes. Keep the force organized. Deny opponents freedom of movement. In the LOCKBOX scenario, PCF's strength lies in maintaining a visible military presence and contesting control of the zone.

“Ground held is leverage gained.”

Operational Priorities

  • Maintain control of designated positions and routes.
  • Protect personnel and critical areas.
  • Challenge competing claims to recovered materiel.
  • Preserve command and unit cohesion under pressure.

Independent Authority

Mandate

ORDER. DUTY. PURPOSE.

Operational Profile // Restricted

Mandate operates in the spaces between official accounts and battlefield reports. Its presence is felt at checkpoints, along closed roads, inside detention sites and wherever information changes hands. Other forces may hold territory; Mandate seeks to control access, decisions and the truth itself.

Doctrine // The Unseen Hand

Mandate is the shadow authority of the operation: disciplined, opaque and deliberately difficult to read. Its people operate under orders that are not always shared, with a chain of purpose that outsiders are not meant to see. A prisoner, a sealed report or a recovered fragment can carry more influence than a fortified position.

“We do not seek power. We uphold what others have forgotten.”

Operational Character

  • Control: Regulate movement and access across the zone.
  • Intelligence: Treat reports, documents and information as operational leverage.
  • Custody: Exercise authority over designated checkpoints and prisoners within event rules.
  • Discretion: Leave rival factions uncertain about Mandate's priorities.

“In chaos, we provide direction. In darkness, we provide light.”

Uniform guidance: The published ruleset does not list a separate authorized camouflage table for Mandate. Confirm Mandate participation and uniform instructions with event staff before preparing your loadout.

Before You Deploy

Faction uniform compliance is governed by the Uniform Standards and Faction Uniform Requirements in the official rules. Headgear and face coverings are faction-color controlled; tactical gear color is unrestricted unless an event-specific rule says otherwise.
